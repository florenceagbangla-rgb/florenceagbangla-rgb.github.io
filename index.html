Background

Nine years across six organisations.

I started at the Ministère du Commerce du Togo in 2017, handling official administrative acts and correspondence. Since then I have worked at Sogesti Informatique — where I set up the company's entire document management system and accounting framework from the ground up — at Moov Togo, at UPS International and at Megatram SARL as assistant to the Director General.

Since 2021 I have been at the CAMEG, Togo's national pharmaceutical procurement agency, managing administrative documentation for a World Bank-funded programme. In parallel, I have developed my digital skills through 16 certified courses and freelance work with clients in India and the United States.

I work best with a written brief, clear deliverables and the autonomy to produce without daily check-ins. I take initiative within the scope I am given and ask when something is unclear rather than guessing.
